# Safety Boundary

PARALLAX Wallet AOS is a proof-governed wallet and transfer runtime scaffold.

It does not perform live money movement. It does not originate ACH files, send wires, issue cards, custody assets, sign blockchain transactions, broadcast transactions, execute trades, provide investment advice, or operate as a licensed money transmitter.

Live mode requires separate activation controls:

1. Legal entity and licensing review.
2. Bank, card, crypto, or ICP partner onboarding.
3. KYC, AML, sanctions, fraud, chargeback, dispute, travel-rule, and jurisdiction controls.
4. Custody and key-management review.
5. Operator approval gates.
6. Immutable receipts and reconciliation.
7. Production incident response and rollback procedures.

Until those gates are complete, every rail in this package is sandbox-only.
